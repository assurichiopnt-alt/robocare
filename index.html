Solutions by care need

Seven care situations, one solutionist

Transfer & Hygiene

Reduce manual lifting for caregivers and keep care receivers clean, dry and comfortable.

Rehabilitation & Mobility

Walking support and training devices, chosen to suit each person under the guidance of their healthcare team.

Staff Protection (WSH)

Wearable exoskeletons that support the back and hips of staff who lift, transfer or bend repeatedly.

Active Aging

Devices that help older adults stay active, get outdoors and move around with more confidence.

AI Humanoid & Service Robots

Humanoid and service robots for assistance, engagement and routine tasks in care facilities.

Positioning & Care Aids

Pillows and everyday aids for positioning, pressure relief and daily care.

iCare Platform

One digital platform connecting care staff, managers and families — care tasks and safety in one place.
