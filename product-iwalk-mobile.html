iWalk Mobile

A two-in-one mobility device: an electric wheelchair that rises into a walking-aid frame with fall-proof, weightless suspension — no manual hauling.

Key features

  • Electric wheelchair mode and walking-aid mode
  • Powered lift from sitting to standing
  • Fall-proof, weightless walking suspension
  • Indoor and outdoor use
