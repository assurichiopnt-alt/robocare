iMan Humanoid

A semi-humanoid robot designed for a wide variety of indoor scenarios — combining large-model AI interaction, human-like arms and an omnidirectional base.

Key features

  • AI large-model interaction for natural conversation
  • Humanoid biomechanical arms with 7 degrees of freedom
  • 24+ degrees of freedom; hollow-leg design for squatting tasks
  • Mecanum wheel base with LiDAR and IMU for omnidirectional movement
  • Dual AI depth vision (4K at 60 Hz)
  • High-capacity battery with charging dock
  • Multiple safety and alert systems
