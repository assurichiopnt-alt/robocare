Multi-function transfer chair

EzLift

A good nursing assistant for caregivers: shower chair, toilet chair, lift chair and wheelchair in one — available in electric-lift and manual-crank versions.

Key features

  • Shower chair, toilet chair, lift chair and wheelchair in one
  • 180° open-and-close seat for easy transfer
  • Electric lifting and lowering — 20 cm adjustable height
  • Medical-grade silent wheels
  • 2.0 mm thick steel frame for stability
  • Manual-crank version available
