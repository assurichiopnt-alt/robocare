iHygiene BedFresh

A 24-hour automatic cleaning system for people receiving care in bed. A smart diaper senses excretion within one second, then the unit washes, dries and purifies the air — with no manual cleaning.

Key features

  • Smart diaper sensor with 1-second auto-sensing
  • Warm-water washing and air drying
  • Air purification: negative ion, UV sterilisation, activated-carbon deodorisation
  • 90° roll-over without leakage
  • Low-noise operation for undisturbed sleep
  • Touch screen and remote controller · clean-water and sewage buckets
