Folda-Go Scooter

A foldable, portable and steady mobility scooter with a patented three-second fold and three modes — driving, folding and trolley.

Key features

  • Patented 3-second folding design
  • Three modes: driving, folding and trolley
  • Powerful motor with electromagnetic brake
  • Four adjustable speed gears
  • Removable lithium battery — two batteries for longer range
  • Oversized LCD display
