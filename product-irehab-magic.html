iRehab Magic Exoskeleton

An intelligent walking aid with AI, sensors and cameras. Two power units at the hip joints assist thigh extension and flexion, adapting to the user’s pace and giving feedback on posture and balance.

Key features

  • Two hip power units assist thigh extension and flexion
  • AI, sensors and cameras adapt to the user’s pace and movement
  • Feedback on posture and balance
  • Designed for people with mobility impairments — under clinical guidance
