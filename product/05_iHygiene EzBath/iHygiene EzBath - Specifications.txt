=== Description / Specifications ===
Portable electric bedside bathing and hair-washing system, specially designed for elderly individuals, post-surgery recovery, bedridden patients, and individuals with limited mobility. It brings a full rinse and shampoo experience directly to the bedside—eliminating the need to transfer the user to a bathroom or traditional shower setup.

Key Features & Highlights

- Compact & Portable Main Unit: Features an integrated top carry handle for effortless transport between rooms, nursing centers, or home-care setups.

- Ergonomic Spray Wand & Hose: Equipped with a flexible accordion hose and a lightweight shower head designed for single-handed caregiver operation.

- Integrated Control Panel: Simple push-button controls located directly on top of the main unit for easy power, water flow, and mode adjustment.

- Drain & Suction Port: Includes a quick-connect side outlet hose attachment to manage water drainage and suction effectively without splashing or spills.

- Clean & Hygienic Design: Constructed from smooth, easy-to-clean molded ABS plastic to withstand daily sanitation and nursing environments.
