This device is equipped with advanced technologies, including AI, sensors, and cameras, to assist users in walking safely and comfortably. These intelligent walking aids are particularly beneficial for individuals with mobility impairments, such as those recovering from strokes or with conditions like Parkinson's disease. By integrating AI, Zuowei's exoskeletons offer a more responsive and personalized rehabilitation experience, enhancing users' independence and confidence in their mobility.
